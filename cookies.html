Cookie policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Synapse sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for the few things below, all needed for the game to work as you set it up. Under UK law (PECR), storage that is strictly necessary doesn't need your consent, so there is nothing to opt into. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, and the fonts are served from this site.

NameWhat it doesHow long
synapse.brainSaves the brain you are building, and its training, so it is there next time.Until you clear it
synapse.nameRemembers the name you play under (vs CPU and Multiplayer), so you don't type it every time.Until you clear it
synapse.noticeRemembers that you've seen the cookie notice, so it doesn't show every time.Until you clear it
synapse.soundRemembers whether you turned the music or sound effects off.Until you clear it
synapse.trackRemembers the track you chose in the sandbox (its family and which one), which Drive and training use.Until you clear it

This storage stays on your device and isn't sent to me. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser's settings; your saved brain will be lost.

Changes

If Synapse ever needs storage that isn't strictly necessary, it will ask first, and this page will be updated.
