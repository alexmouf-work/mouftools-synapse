# vs CPU as a 5-round match; signed speed and distance; a small first budget (2026-11-05)

Principal, 2026-11-05: "make the speed cell return a negative output if moving in a negative direction. count negative
distance as being negative." · "build the 5 rounds that we planned, with their appropriate timers. each race should be
1 minute." · "energy budget starts really small, allowing for maybe 8-10 neurons total including inputs." Then, asked
for round 1's build length and how to score: "60s build for first round, followed by 10s train and 30s race, then 20s
build, 10s train with 30s race for each round following that." · Scoring: "Total distance".
(The later answer sets the race length at 30 s, replacing "each race should be 1 minute".) Original ruling 2026-10-30:
5 rounds per game; weights carry over between rounds; the energy budget grows every round.

## 1. The match (vs CPU now; Multiplayer follows as its own stage)
| Round | Build | Train | Race |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 s (from scratch) | 10 s | 30 s |
| 2–5 | 20 s (upgrade the same brain) | 10 s | 30 s |
- **Timer:** every phase has the big top-centre countdown from the build-timer plan
  (`2026-11-05-build-timer-and-cell-minus.md`), labelled "Round n of 5 · Build" / "Train" / "Race". It turns red under
  10 s (under 5 s for the 10 s phases) and pulses with a tick in the last 5 s. Build at 0 starts Train itself. Train
  runs for its 10 s: it spends the fixed budget T, its view holds until the 10 s are up, then the race starts. The
  race ends at 30 s.
- **Carry-over:** each round's training starts from the brain that came out of the last round's training (the
  champion, then its mutants, for evolution). Weights of cells added in the upgrade start at 0; removed cells drop
  their weights. Temperature stays ×1.
- **Race:** 10 cars on the grid and the countdown, as now, but 30 s long with no laps to finish. The score is the
  distance covered in those 30 s: signed, so going backwards counts against you. The wrong-way reset still applies
  (ruling 2026-11-05), so a backwards car only loses ground until it is put back.
- **Standings:** after each race, a table of that race's distances and the running total. The match winner is the
  greatest total distance after round 5, and the winner music plays if that's you. "Next round" (or the build timer
  starting by itself after about 8 s) moves on; after round 5 the final standings show with Play again / Back.
- **Each round draws a new track** from any family, seeded from the match seed.
- **CPU brains:** nine CPU players, each with a design that fits each round's budget and the brain carried round to
  round. They are generated offline by the script (scripts/sim/cpuBrains.ts) per round under that round's budget,
  stored as JSON, with skill spread as now.

## 2. Energy budget per round
- The match budget is the most a design may use at flat out. It starts small: round 1 fits about 8–10 cells in total,
  inputs included (for example 3–4 inputs and 5–6 hidden neurons, fully wired, plus the 4 wheels). It grows each
  round in the planned proportions 10 : 14 : 20 : 26 : 32 (×1, ×1.4, ×2, ×2.6, ×3.2).
- Calibrate R1 in config.ts so that a design of 9 cells (4 inputs + 5 tanh hidden, fully wired, 4 wheels) just fits,
  and 11 doesn't. State the resulting percentages.
- The designer's energy bar shows the round's budget as its full scale in match mode. A change that would go over is
  refused, as now. The +s hide when nothing more fits.
- The sandbox stays as it is: no limit.

## 3. Signed speed cell and signed distance (sim; every mode)
- The speed cell reads the car's signed forward speed: negative when moving backwards. Its response stays the same
  shape, mirrored for negative speeds.
- Distance is signed everywhere it's shown or scored: race results, standings, the drive view and Multiplayer
  results. It's no longer clamped at 0.
- Goldens change: say why in the commit. Re-run S1 for evolution and report it; don't tune.

## Built (2026-11-05, landed together with the sidebar plan)
- **Match** (`src/client/ui/rounds/rounds.ts`): Title → vs CPU starts round 1 in the designer, from the starting brain
  (`defaultSpec`, untrained). Each phase has the big countdown (`ui/phaseTimer.ts`): "Round n of 5 · Build" (60 s,
  then 20 s; red under 10 s; at 0 Train starts itself), "Round n of 5 · Train" (10 s, red under 5 s; T is spent at
  once and the view holds; the race starts when both the 10 s are up and training is done), "Round n of 5 · Race"
  (30 s from Go). The standings show this race's signed distance and the total per player, best total first, with
  "Round n+1 of 5 in" counting 8 s and Next round. After round 5: the final standings, Play again / Back, and the
  winner music if you lead. Each round's track: `derive(matchSeed, 0x50d5, round)`.
- **Carry-over:** under a match rule the edit ops keep every wire and bias whose cells are still there
  (`brain.ts carryWeights`; added cells at 0, removed cells' weights dropped; a layer inserted or removed shifts the
  others' ids). The sandbox still resets weights on every edit (ruling 2026-11-04). Training in round n is phase n − 1
  of the match's training seed, from the brain as built (`start: carry` once it has weights), temperature ×1.
- **vs CPU's brain is its own:** it is edited in the designer but never saved over the sandbox brain (the sandbox brain
  is back when the match ends). Reset in a match gives the starting brain (a random starter could exceed round 1).
- **Budgets** (`CONFIG.rounds.budgets`): 20, 28, 40, 52, 64% flat out. 4 boundary cells + 5 tanh neurons (9 cells,
  fully wired, 4 wheels) use 19.5%; 11 such cells use 23.6–23.7% (refused). Cheaper kinds fit more (e.g. head
  direction cells and linear neurons): the budget is energy, not a cell count. The starting brain uses 11.35%. The
  designer's energy bar uses the round's budget as its full scale ("Brain 4–11% of 20%"), and every "+" (tray, neuron,
  layer, per boundary) hides when that edit wouldn't fit.
- **Race:** `runRace(…, ROUND_RACE)`: 600 ticks, no laps; `RaceCar.dist` is the signed distance covered from the
  car's grid slot (the grid's rows start up to 36 m apart, so progress from the line would not be fair), and the
  ranking is by it. Multiplayer keeps 2 laps in 120 s.
- **Signed:** the speed cell reads the signed forward speed (−1..1, same shape mirrored); it costs energy by its size
  either way (`brain.ts inRate`), and its gain gradient covers negative speeds. Distances are signed in race results,
  standings, the racers list, the drive view and the room's reported result ("−0.05 km"; "0.00 km" never takes a sign).
- **CPU brains** (`scripts/sim/cpuBrains.ts`, 3.6 min with 4 workers): four growth styles (eyes, even, pace, deep),
  each player's design grown with the player's edit ops while the next step fits the round's budget, trained with
  evolution on its share of T (5%–100%) from the last round's brain. Median 30 s distance alone on ten race tracks is
  printed per brain (about 0.6–1.4 km); not tuned.
- **Calls made:** round 1 starts from `defaultSpec` (fits 20%), not the sandbox brain; the race timer starts at Go
  (after the 5…1 countdown); the training timer's 10 s count from when Train is pressed (a slow device that has not
  finished by then races as soon as it has); the rear wing's clearcoat is softer (its flat top mirrored a low sun past
  the bloom threshold on a new race seed's grid).
