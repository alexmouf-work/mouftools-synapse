## The track
One track, 4 min 35 s, 151.99 BPM, first downbeat at 0.200 s, key F♯ minor / D major. Its form repeats every 80 bars:
a quiet intro (bars 0–7), full-energy sections (8–71) and a quiet breakdown (72–78) ending on a hit (79) that leads back
into the intro. Cut into ten 8-bar phrases (12.6 s each) on the bar grid, each with one beat before and two after so
joins can cross-fade:

| Pool | Phrases (source bars) |
|---|---|
| Building (low energy) | intro (0–7), breakdown (72–79) |
| Racing (high energy) | drive-a/b (8–23), lift-a/b (24–39), push-a/b (40–55), soar-a/b (56–71) |

## More tracks (2026-11-04; licences confirmed by the principal)
- **Sandbox building: "Airport Lounge"** (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, credited on the terms page and the workbench).
  130 BPM; a 128-bar loop (16.0 s to 252.3 s of the original, chosen at phrase lines with matching spectra) that
  repeats on itself. The DM DOKURO intro and breakdown stay as the building music for vs CPU and Multiplayer lobbies.
- **Winner music**: plays once when the player wins a race, then returns to the mode's music.
- Different tempo and key from the race track, so lounge ↔ race cross-fade (about 1.5–2 s) rather than beat-match; the
  countdown still follows the race track's clock.
- Levels matched to −16 LUFS by per-track gain: race −9.0 dB, lounge −0.2 dB, winner −4.1 dB.

## How it plays
- Web Audio, one phrase after another, scheduled a second ahead on the bar grid: music never stops.
- Phrases that were neighbours in the original join seamlessly (a 24 ms splice); jumps cross-fade over one beat,
  centred on the downbeat. Same tempo and key throughout, so every join lands on the beat.
- Building loops breakdown → intro → breakdown (the breakdown's hit leads into the intro as in the original). Racing
  runs drive → lift → push → soar and back to drive.
- **To race:** on the next bar the music jumps into the intro's build-up (bar 4); the countdown ticks 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
  every two beats (0.79 s) from bar 6, beat 3, and **Go lands on the drop**, where the intro runs into the racing
  sections. Request to Go: 6–8 s; the countdown itself: 4 s.
- **Back to building:** cross-fades into the breakdown at the next half-phrase (4-bar) line.
- The countdown follows the music, so the race start waits for the beat (≤ 1 bar). Multiplayer will start every
  player's race from the same server time; the music still lands Go locally.

## Countdown and car (synthesised, no files)
- Countdown: short blips on F♯5 for 5…1, and Go as an F♯–A–C♯ chord an octave up: in key with the track.
- The cars are electric, a motor per wheel: a motor whine that rises with wheel speed, road roar that grows with
  speed, and tyre squeal when one side's wheels turn faster than the other's (skid-steer cornering). Drive view: your
  car; training: the highlighted car only; races: yours plus the nearest few, quieter with distance.

## As built
- Mapping: the sandbox workbench plays the lounge; the training and drive views play the race music. The DM DOKURO
  build pool (intro ↔ breakdown) is in the engine for the lobbies to come.
- Lounge → race: the intro starts at bar 4 at once (no beat-matching across tempos) while the lounge fades out over
  1.5 s, equal-power; Go is the drop, 8 − 4 bars later (about 6.4 s). Race → lounge: at the next bar line, a 2 s
  cross-fade into the lounge's loop start. The lounge joins itself with a one-beat cross-fade.
- Winner: `win()` cross-fades it in over 0.5 s, plays it once, then cross-fades back into the mode's music. Nothing
  calls it yet (no wins until vs CPU and Multiplayer); `check:audio` calls it.
- The countdown is in the drive view (open and New track), big Arial bold numbers centred over the scene. The car waits
  on the start line until Go and then the recorded run plays from tick 0, so drive hashes are unchanged. Sound on: it
  follows the race music; locked or muted: the same spacing (two beats) on a plain timer. The numbers change on their
  own timers, so slow frames never skip one.
- Engine: drive view, your car (speed from the sim, squeal from the wheel outputs: |left − right|); training view, the
  solid best car at about a third of the level (speed, and squeal from its lateral demand against the grip, since
  training paths keep no wheel outputs).
- Mute: a speaker button (square corners, `aria-pressed` = sound on, label "Sound") in the workbench header and both
  views' action rows; remembered in `synapse.sound` (strictly necessary; on the cookie page; notice version 2).
- Audio starts on the first tap, click or key anywhere (browsers require a gesture); the files are fetched after first
  paint (the lounge first) and decoded once unlocked. A hidden tab suspends the audio.
- **Rights:** confirmed by the principal on 2026-11-04 (DM DOKURO: permission from the artist; "Airport Lounge": CC BY
  4.0; winner music: licensed). Credits on the terms page and the workbench's legal line.

## Title onwards (2026-11-04)
"it should keep playing music continuously from the title onwards, smoothly but quickly transitioning between tracks
… it should keep playing even when not focused."
- Music starts with the first tap or key on the title (browsers allow nothing sooner) and never stops after that.
- The title plays the race track's quiet pool (intro, breakdown); Sandbox → the lounge; races → the race sections.
- Cross-fades between different tracks shorten to about 0.6 s, equal-power, on the next beat of the outgoing track.
- No suspending in a hidden or unfocused tab; mute is the only way to silence it.
- Leaving a race stops its sounds (2026-11-04: "make sure to stop racing sound effects when leaving the racing part of
  the site."): every way out of the training and drive views (Back, Keep training, Drive, the title link, a hidden
  view) stops the engines and cancels any countdown blips still scheduled. Only the music carries on.
