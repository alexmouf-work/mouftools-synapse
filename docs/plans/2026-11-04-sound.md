# Sound: music that follows the game, the countdown, the car (plan; audition page built)

Principal (2026-11-04): music for the racing, "chop it up into bits so that we can play it at the correct times. We need
music playing at all times, and it needs to transition very smoothly. we can repeat sections. Use the high energy
parts for the racing segments and the low energy parts for the building segments." · "add sound effects for \"5, 4,
3, 2, 1, go\"." · "add sound effects for the car driving too, which will play appropriately for how fast the car is
driving."

Audition page: `reference/sound-test.html` (the audio files are not in the repo; see Rights).

## The track
One track, 4 min 35 s, 151.99 BPM, first downbeat at 0.200 s, key F♯ minor / D major. Its form repeats every 80 bars:
a quiet intro (bars 0–7), full-energy sections (8–71) and a quiet breakdown (72–78) ending on a hit (79) that leads back
into the intro. Cut into ten 8-bar phrases (12.6 s each) on the bar grid, each with one beat before and two after so
joins can cross-fade:

| Pool | Phrases (source bars) |
|---|---|
| Building (low energy) | intro (0–7), breakdown (72–79) |
| Racing (high energy) | drive-a/b (8–23), lift-a/b (24–39), push-a/b (40–55), soar-a/b (56–71) |

## More tracks (2026-11-04; licences confirmed by the principal)
- **Sandbox building: "Airport Lounge"** (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, credited on the terms page and the workbench).
  130 BPM; a 128-bar loop (16.0 s to 252.3 s of the original, chosen at phrase lines with matching spectra) that
  repeats on itself. The DM DOKURO intro and breakdown stay as the building music for vs CPU and Multiplayer lobbies.
- **Winner music**: plays once when the player wins a race, then returns to the mode's music.
- Different tempo and key from the race track, so lounge ↔ race cross-fade (about 1.5–2 s) rather than beat-match; the
  countdown still follows the race track's clock.
- Levels matched to −16 LUFS by per-track gain: race −9.0 dB, lounge −0.2 dB, winner −4.1 dB.

## How it plays
- Web Audio, one phrase after another, scheduled a second ahead on the bar grid: music never stops.
- Phrases that were neighbours in the original join seamlessly (a 24 ms splice); jumps cross-fade over one beat,
  centred on the downbeat. Same tempo and key throughout, so every join lands on the beat.
- Building loops breakdown → intro → breakdown (the breakdown's hit leads into the intro as in the original). Racing
  runs drive → lift → push → soar and back to drive.
- **To race:** on the next bar the music jumps into the intro's build-up (bar 4); the countdown ticks 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
  every two beats (0.79 s) from bar 6, beat 3, and **Go lands on the drop**, where the intro runs into the racing
  sections. Request to Go: 6–8 s; the countdown itself: 4 s.
- **Back to building:** cross-fades into the breakdown at the next half-phrase (4-bar) line.
- The countdown follows the music, so the race start waits for the beat (≤ 1 bar). Multiplayer will start every
  player's race from the same server time; the music still lands Go locally.

## Countdown and car (synthesised, no files)
- Countdown: short blips on F♯5 for 5…1, and Go as an F♯–A–C♯ chord an octave up: in key with the track.
- The cars are electric, a motor per wheel: a motor whine that rises with wheel speed, road roar that grows with
  speed, and tyre squeal when one side's wheels turn faster than the other's (skid-steer cornering). Drive view: your
  car; training: the highlighted car only; races: yours plus the nearest few, quieter with distance.
