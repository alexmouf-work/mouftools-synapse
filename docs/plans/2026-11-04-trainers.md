# Synapse — the other trainers: plan and results

Principal, 2026-11-04: "build in the other training methods. show on screen the delta rule being used, at all times."
Order (2026-10-30): after evolution, backprop (copy a teacher), actor-critic, A_RP. Every trainer gets the same
experience budget T (car-ticks), so they compete on sample efficiency. The seam is solo-loop plan §3b; what exists is in
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Trainers". Scope: trainers and the on-screen learning rule only (not brain designer v2).

## Stages (each: checks green, a commit, docs in it, then stop for review)
- **T1 backprop**: a scripted pure-pursuit teacher (`sim/teacher.ts`); the brain drives, the teacher labels each state
  (DAgger); the generalised delta rule. Channels signal + delta. Refuses step neurons.
- **T2 actor-critic**: a critic owned by the trainer estimates V(s); TD error δ = r + γV(s′) − V(s), r = progress this
  tick minus wall touches; Gaussian exploration on the wheels; actor Δw = α·δ·∂log π/∂w, critic Δv = β·δ·∇V. Channels
  signal + value + td. Refuses step neurons.
- **T3 A_RP**: stochastic output units; reward 1 for progress without a wall touch, else penalty; Δw = ρ·(y − E[y])·x on
  reward, λρ·(−y − E[y])·x on penalty. Channels signal + reward.
- **T4 the learning rule on screen**: principal 2026-11-04, "show only the formula for the delta rule, no calculation
  directly happening. don't show the neuron selected in that area either. centre the delta rule, and directly call it
  \"Learning Rule : [equation]\"" → one centred strip across the top on the workbench, training and drive views, exactly
  "Learning Rule : <the active trainer's rule>", no numbers and no selected wire; the trainer picker becomes live.

Checks per trainer: `contract.test.ts`; a unit test of its update against a hand-computed step on a tiny net; the S1
bench (`npm run bench -- --trainer <id>`: seeds 1–20, Starter, same T, same 60 s episodes and pool), reporting
(a)–(d), (f) and the phase time. Gate: fitness improves on ≥ 15/20 seeds (end ≥ 1.5× start). (a)/(b) are reported,
not gated. Training must fit the 10 s phase on the reference desktop.

Backprop's start is one random brain (fitness ≈ 0), so its ratios are huge and say little; the absolute numbers are
the comparison. On the same T it ends about as good as evolution on the training tracks and on the unseen track, and
better with Minimal (straight regression on the eyes). It is less reliable: 2 of 20 seeds stay stuck, and the score
from one check to the next can swing (a run that drove 3 000 m can drop to 400 m after one update, then recover),
which is the lecture's "gradient descent … can run into local minima" made visible. It is slower per car-tick than
evolution: its cars drive well from early on (long eye rays are the dearest part of a tick), every tick also runs the
teacher and a backward pass, and all lanes wait for the slowest one before each update (a synchronous step, as the
brief expected). It fits the 10 s phase here in Chrome; to confirm on the reference desktop with
`/bench/?trainer=backprop`.

What the failures were: (1) the teacher's steering at speed asks for a wheel difference of ~0.01, below what the brain's
regression resolves, so the copy steered at random and pinned itself to a wall: a firmer teacher (gain 3, steering
computed for at least 40 m/s) fixed it and drives no worse alone (no wall touch on 20/20, 30 s median 1 565 m). (2) Dead
ReLU: Starter has no bias wire, its cruise neuron is a ReLU, and with a negative draw on Energy left it never fires, so
its slope is 0 forever; positive starting weights into a ReLU fixed it. (3) Longer rounds trade a little reliability
(8/8 → 7/8 on seeds 1–8) for a phase that fits the time limit.

Changed from the brief, and why:
- The teacher is pure pursuit with a steering gain of 3 and a 40 m/s floor on the speed it steers for (above).
- Lanes start each episode at a seeded place on a training track (not the start line), at rest, so 16 cars sharing
  weights do not drive the same path; the fitness checks still start on the line, as evolution's episodes do.
- DAgger's mixing kept (an episode is teacher-driven with probability β, 0.5 → 0 by 25% of T); every tick the teacher
  drives is a metered car-tick like any other. Labelling a state costs nothing.
- The wheels' clamp passes the error when it points back into range (its true slope there is 0).
- weights() is the current weights (no best-checkpoint); the final check scores them.
- Linear joins the transfer functions and step is no longer offered (principal 2026-11-04), folded into T1 at the
  coordinator's request: linear = z kept to −1..1, f′ = 1 inside, step's old upkeep; old saves' step neurons load as
  Linear with a one-time note. Starter and the other presets are unchanged (no step in them; S1 numbers stand).

## T2 actor-critic and T3 A_RP: built (B4, 2026-11-04)
Both are gradient trainers on the shared frame (16 lanes, 400-tick rounds, a fitness check every 150k car-ticks or per
sandbox generation, the same T), each with its own rollout kernel (`acKernel.ts`, `arpKernel.ts`), so the Node and
browser pools run them unchanged. Both are live in the trainer picker; Temperature scales each one's exploration
(actor-critic's σ, A_RP's firing randomness); both learn the biases and the cells' fields that have a gradient (the
error reaches the sense cells by the delta rule, then `fieldgrad.ts`), as backprop does: on the default brain the
head-direction angle moves, the boundary cell's direction and width stay (no gradient). Details in
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Trainers".
- **Actor-critic**: the lanes drive a = y + σ·ε per wheel (σ 0.3 → 0.05 over T, × temperature); a linear critic over
  [1, every input cell, every hidden neuron, speed / top speed]; r = progress − 0.5 × wall touch per tick (the training
  fitness per tick); δ = r + γV(s′) − V(s), γ 0.98, V = 0 after an episode ends; TD(λ) traces, λ 0.9; actor
  Δw = α·Σδ·e/n (α 0.1, e the trace of ∂log π/∂w with ∂log π/∂y = ε/σ), critic Δv = β·Σδ·e_v/n (β 1).
- **A_RP**: each wheel a ±1 unit, P(+1) = (1 + E[y])/2 with E[y] = tanh(z) (its race output); the lanes drive
  E[y] + s·(y − E[y]) (s 0.75, × temperature); reward = progress without a wall touch this tick; δ = y − E[y] on
  reward, λ(−y − E[y]) on penalty (λ 0.2), which is ∂log P(y)/∂z, so no slope at the wheels; Δw = ρ·Σδ·x/n (ρ 0.5).

Read: the default brain can't see the road (one boundary cell 20 m ahead and a compass), which is what holds back
the gradient trainers, as with backprop (B2b). On a brain that can see, actor-critic comes close to evolution and A_RP
reaches about half of it (table above). On the default brain A_RP is the best of the three gradient trainers. Its
reinforcement is one bit per tick and its spikes are big, so it learns "keep moving, stay off the walls" and drives
cleanly (no wall ticks) but slowly (no reward for speed). Actor-critic's critic sees almost nothing on this brain,
so the TD error is mostly noise. Its noisy lanes do make progress (about 0.7 m per tick in training), but the
noise-free policy it leaves often drives into a wall and stays there, or reverses. Both fit the 10 s phase here,
in Node and in Chrome.

Levers tried (seeds 1–6 or 1–8, final training fitness median; "≥ 500" counts seeds):
- Actor-critic, default brain: α 0.02 with reward ×0.25 (learns forward, far too slowly: |Δw| ~1e-3 per round) →
  α 0.03–0.3 with reward in metres (medians −17 to 37; peaks of 150–400 that don't last); a constant σ left a
  policy that only worked with its noise (noisy lanes 0.7 m/tick, noise-free −20), so σ now falls over T
  (as evolution's σ does), which helped a little; σ 0.5, γ 0.95/λ 0.8, β 0–3, γ = λ = 0, the critic without speed: all
  medians ≤ 37, 0/8 ≥ 500. A check of the estimator (critic 0, every weight 0) gives the expected positive gradient on
  the wheels' biases; the seeds that fail drift there through the critic (e.g. a negative weight on speed rewarding
  reversing). Kept α 0.1, β 1, σ 0.3 → 0.05, γ 0.98, λ 0.9, chosen where it works (the sighted brain).
- A_RP, default brain: λ 0.05 → median 300 (2/8); λ 0.2 → 815 (7/8); λ 0.3 → 762; λ 0.5 → 765; ρ 1 → 560; s 0.25 →
  123, s 1 → median 564 with peaks to 1986 but unstable; **λ 0.2, s 0.75, ρ 0.5 → 1181 (6/8)**, built.

Changed from the brief, and why:
- Actor-critic: eligibility traces (TD(λ)) on both actor and critic, so that a wall touch is blamed on the steering
  that led to it (one-step TD alone, γ = λ = 0, measured no better); σ anneals over T; the critic reads the car's
  speed besides the brain's own cells and neurons (the critic is the trainer's, not part of the brain); the
  transition open at a round's end is dropped (1 tick in 400).
- A_RP: only the wheels are stochastic units, as the brief says, and their error reaches the hidden layers by the
  delta rule. A_RP units in every layer, all sharing the one reward bit, would be the other way to do it; I didn't
  build that. Training drives the unit's mean plus s × its spike's deviation, not the raw ±1 spike, so the
  temperature has something to scale (s = 1 would drive the spike itself). The rule strip keeps the B3 formula
  `Δw = ρ·(±y − p)·x`.
- Two kernel hooks (`laneStart`, `episodeEnd`) and an option for `grad.ts` to take the error at z. Backprop's and
  evolution's goldens are unchanged.
