Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Synapse is a free game made by Alexander Moufarrige ("I", "me"). I am the data controller for Synapse. You can reach me at alexander@enkefalos.co.uk.

The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your brains stay on your device.

What is collected, and why

  • Your brains, on your device. What you build is saved in your browser's local storage so it is there next time, with the name you play under and the track you chose. None of it is sent to me (your name goes to a room only when you join one; see Matches). See the cookie policy.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it briefly logs requests (IP address, browser, the page asked for, time) to deliver the site and keep it secure. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • Matches. When you play a match, the room code, the display name you choose, your brain (its design and weights) and the race result are sent to Supabase, which stores them so every player's device can run the same race. They are deleted within 30 days. Lawful basis: providing the game you asked to play (contract). Don't use your real name as a display name if you'd rather not share it.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to profile you.

Who handles it

Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (matches) process data for me under data processing agreements. Some of their servers are outside the UK; where so, transfers are protected by UK adequacy regulations or the UK's standard contractual safeguards.

How long it is kept

Brains on your device: until you delete them or clear your browser's site data. Server logs: as long as Vercel keeps them, typically a few days. Match data: up to 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to have a copy of it. Email alexander@enkefalos.co.uk and I'll reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Synapse is fine for all ages. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before playing matches.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
